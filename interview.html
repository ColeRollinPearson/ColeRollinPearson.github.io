Describe a technical problem that you solved.

  • In process test for residual sodium hypochlorite began failing consistently, putting a halt in our production.
  • Used batch records to collect information and determine what each failed test had in common.
  • Used test result documentation to establish a timeline and chronological trend for the failed test results.
  • Observed production methods and in process testing methods and materials, discovered that the incorrect reagent had been used for conducting in process testing.
  • Established root cause - material spec sheet for new test kits did not include inspection of the included reagent part number, part number was only present for the test kit itself.
  • Generated NCR, CAPA, and new material spec sheet, and spearheaded training procedures and implementation and review of the CAPA.

How have you used statistical analysis in the past?

  • ANOVA Test: An In-Depth Guide with Examples | DataCamp.
  • Capstone project; we performed ANOVA calculations for our quantitative tests to optimize our prototype.
  • Used ANOVA to compare results of different computational algorithms for determining position from acceleration data.
  • Used paired t-test for analyzing changes to one algorithm step by step.
  • Used two way ANOVA to compare several different base algorithms each with two distinct phases of computation.
  • At Stability I spearheaded a data collection program to create performance metrics for my team and used paired t-tests to establish evidence of performance improvement. I recommended a performance bonus incentive based on this data.
    • Used this information to make data-driven decisions regarding delegation of responsibilities.
    • There was a lack of performance metrics when I joined the team.

  • I have worked extensively with document control, labeling control, and process controls within these frameworks.
  • I have written document change requests, non conformance reports, validation protocol reports, IQ OQ PQ reports, and I have spearheaded process optimizations involving validation of process changes within the quality management system.
  • I also worked on applying six sigma lean principles to make continuous improvements to the quality control processes with changes such as new pull systems, redesigned work areas, and more efficient communication strategies.

How would you handle a situation where a product failed to meet its performance specifications?

  • Quarantine the product and observe processes as well as the quality control systems in place.
  • I would analyze testing data from previous lots to determine if there was any detectable trend and what the context was in terms of how long that trend was going on and how significantly the results changed.
  • I would open the design history file and determine if the failure mode was consistent with anything contained in the risk probability matrix or failure mode and effects analysis.
  • If none of this information led to a conclusive fix then I would implement more in process evaluations earlier in the process to determine where the failure mode was being introduced and I would document in process evaluations heavily.
  • If no solution was arrived at, I would reassess the risk priority number and design history file and see if there was a different way to achieve the required performance specification.

What are your strategies for managing and prioritizing multiple projects simultaneously?

  • I like to use priority matrices and inter-project gantt charts to plan my management of multiple projects.
  • I use work breakdown structures to define tasks into subtasks until I feel as though I can predict the amount of time a task will take, then I place those subtasks in chronological order within a gantt chart.
  • I use a priority matrix to determine in which order I should complete tasks that can be done simultaneously.
  • In my current management context I use the priority matrix to determine what I can delegate and who I can delegate it to.

Please describe your experience with root cause analysis and corrective action processes.

  • In my current role I have carried out root cause analysis using data trend and batch records to determine when a problem began happening and what the problem results all have in common. One example is my experience with the determination that my team was using the incorrect reagent for an in process laboratory test. I used batch records to determine what they all had in common, then I used our QC internal data collection to determine when the incorrect reagent was introduced, which is how I came to the conclusion that the incorrect reagent was introduced at the same time that we received a brand new test kit.

How have you ensured that a product adheres to all relevant safety and regulatory standards in a previous role?

  • I maintain an active continuing education program for me and my team members to stay up to date on relevant safety and regulatory requirements. This includes reviewing our applicable standards yearly and including the discussion of any updated standards in our team meetings.

Can you provide an example of a time when you had to make a difficult decision that had a significant impact on product sustainability?

  • There was an occasion where the quality assurance team came to us because they were missing testing records for some our lots, and they requested that we generate new records for them. My team was unable to verify that the testing had ever been conducted for those lots, and ultimately I had to tell the quality assurance team that no record existed and the lots would need to be scrapped and an NCR generated. This was during a very busy growth phase where we were already not meeting customer demand and this decision put all of the departments in a difficult position in terms of conducting root cause analysis to see who was at fault. Ultimately the process through which manufacturing submits in process testing samples was changed.

How would you deal with a situation where a product design needed to be changed due to unforeseen issues?

  • I would generate a new design specification based on the issues that were discovered. I would start by creating design inputs based on the new issue and discuss with other departments or with customers to determine if the user needs or intended use of the device needed to be adjusted. I would design a testing protocol to assess whether the changes made to the device satisfy the new design inputs.

Can you discuss a time when you used data analysis to improve a product's performance or reliability?

  • Product will meet all specifications pre-sterilization but comes back and does not pass the post-sterilization evaluation requirements.
  • We have analyzed the donor records and established a solid trend relating donor characteristics to the lots that fail post-ster evaluation.
  • We are currently still developing new processing procedures to account for variation in donor characteristics based on the trend we have established.
