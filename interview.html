Questions & Answers

Here I have compiled some of my common interview answers.

What is your experience with design engineering?

Design engineering was the main focus of my last year of undergraduate education. My capstone project was to develop a novel medical device prototype using design engineering tools and principles. My team and I created a design process map complete with user needs, design inputs, design outputs, verification of outputs to inputs and validation of user needs to outputs, and the design process control and map were all documented in a design history file consistent with 21 CFR 820 and ISO 13485 regulations. The device was a ureteroscope navigation system based on MEMS accelerometers and pressure sensors that tracked the movement and forces associated with a ureteroscopy procedure. The acceleration data is used to determine the devices position in 3D space and the pressure sensor can alert the operator of excessive forces.

What is your experience with software development?

I have experience with software development in both academic and personal projects. In my capstone project, I developed the software for the ureteroscope navigation system using C++ and Python. The software was responsible for reading data from the MEMS sensors, processing the data to determine the device's position and forces, and providing real-time feedback to the operator. I also have experience with web development, as evidenced by this portfolio website which was built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
