Quality Control Supervisor

  • Applied design control processes and drove continuous improvement for human biological material (tissue allografts) manufacturing in a sterile environment
  • Directed quality control and manufacturing operations at a certified tissue bank, ensuring all activities stayed fully compliant with 21 CFR 1271, 21 CFR 820, AATB Standards, and ISO 13485
  • Implemented Six Sigma lean principles in an FDA-regulated manufacturing setting and upheld current good manufacturing practices (cGMP)
  • Managed and organized batch records and laboratory information, and consolidated datasets to monitor key trends and strengthen root cause analysis
  • Conducted laboratory procedures and processed bone and birth tissues using aseptic techniques
  • Performed routine in-process and stability testing on donor tissues and finished products
  • Planned and executed environmental monitoring activities, including plating and particle counts
  • Built and deployed automated product-labeling workflows using BarTender
  • Trained new and current personnel to maintain ongoing competency and up-to-date knowledge of standard operating protocols (SOPs)
  • Managed and led a team of up to 20 quality control and manufacturing personnel, achieving 50% productivity gains while upholding quality standards
